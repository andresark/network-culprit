DNS failures are easier to spot and investigate:

- The DNS card highlights failed resolver checks instead of leading with a potentially cached lookup time.
- A unique query through the native macOS resolver checks for a fresh DNS response without flushing caches or changing network settings.
- Opening a dashboard card starts a lightweight check. Its detail sheet updates when results arrive, shows the measurement time, and includes Check Again.
- Failing rows appear first. Historical checks retain their original results and timestamps.
- Repeated SERVFAIL, NXDOMAIN, REFUSED, or empty answers for the configured test hostname are recorded as failed queries. Partial and slow responses remain warnings.
- Mixed results identify the failing DNS paths without declaring an entire provider down when encrypted or VPN DNS still works.

Requires macOS 27 or later and Apple Silicon. The free build is ad hoc signed and not Apple notarized. Existing diagnostic history, monitoring settings, login registration, and notification preferences are retained when updating the installed app.
